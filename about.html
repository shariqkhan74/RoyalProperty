Our Mission & Approach

Rooted in Bareilly. Focused on Real Value.

Royal Property is a Bareilly-based real estate advisory and land development firm led by Shakir Ali Khan. We specialize in evaluating raw and agricultural land parcels, planning structured residential layouts, developing basic site infrastructure, and assisting customers in finding suitable residential plots and newly constructed houses.

Our philosophy is rooted in practical local market knowledge and honest, straightforward communication. Rather than making exaggerated claims or promoting speculative developments, we focus on ground realities: verified accessibility, planned internal roads, clear demarcations, and genuine legal transparency.

From the bustling connectivity of Nakatia and Nariyawal to the serene residential belts of Mohnapur and Thiriya Nizawat Khan, we help families and investors make informed property decisions.

Comprehensive Services

What Royal Property Does

We provide a complete spectrum of land development, plotting, and housing services tailored to Bareilly’s local requirements.

1. Agricultural Land Buying

Identifying and purchasing promising agricultural land parcels situated around growing arterial roads of Bareilly.

2. Planned Land Development

Developing suitable land parcels into organized residential properties where legally permitted under local regulations.

3. Internal Road & Site Infrastructure

Constructing internal road networks, leveling plots, and establishing basic on-site infrastructure for clean layout access.

4. Residential Plots in Various Sizes

Offering demarcated small, medium, and large residential plots structured to fit individual homeowner blueprints.

5. New House Construction

Building modern independent residential houses on suitable developed layouts for turnkey family living.

6. Land & Home Finding Assistance

Helping buyers navigate property options, verify local zoning, and choose the right location across Bareilly.

Transparency & Legal Compliance Statement

Land development and plotting services are subject to applicable approvals, land-use permissions, title verification, and local regulations. Royal Property does not claim that every agricultural property is automatically converted into residential plots. Property availability and development remain subject to statutory verification and local laws.
