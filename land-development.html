Step-by-Step Lifecycle

The 6-Phase Development Process

How we take raw land and turn it into structured, accessible, and ready-to-build residential plots in Bareilly.

01

Land Identification

Carefully locating agricultural and open land parcels in emerging growth corridors of Bareilly (such as near Nakatia, Nariyawal, and Mohnapur) with practical connectivity to major transport routes.

02

Site Evaluation

Conducting ground topography surveys, examining soil firmness, confirming roadway width access, and performing document verification before initiating any site commitments.

03

Planning & Development

Creating master layout blueprints that allocate adequate road widths, ensure corner plot visibility, structure entry corridors, and plan optimal plot orientations.

04

Road & Layout Development

Executing heavy earth leveling, sub-base preparation, internal asphalt or concrete road paving, curb installation, and dedicated utility corridor planning.

05

Plot Planning

Dividing and physically demarcating individual plots across small, medium, and large size tiers with boundary markers and numbered plot indicators.

06

Property Sales & Possession

Assisting purchasers through transparent documentation, on-site physical possession handover, and clear registry/deed processes in accordance with local regulations.

Regulatory & Statutory Compliance Notice

All development activities and plotting services are subject to applicable land-use permissions, local authority approvals, title verification, and statutory regulations. Royal Property does not claim that every agricultural property is automatically converted into residential plots. Property availability and development remain subject to statutory verification, permissions, and applicable laws.

Road Construction & Site Quality

Access roads are the foundation of any thriving residential community. In all our development sites across Bareilly, we prioritize adequate road widths that allow seamless two-way vehicular movement, service access, and smooth connectivity to main link roads.
